How to interpret the comparisons

The supplied chart is a planning estimate. The shaded range comes from published age guides and uses age at the start of the selected week. A result within that range alone cannot establish that an increase is appropriate for Violet.

Offer feeds in response to hunger and stop when she shows fullness. The AAP gives a usual general daily reference of about 960 mL, with individual needs discussed with her pediatrician.
