    Sleep and play

    For sleep, place her on her back on a firm, flat surface with an empty sleep space. Tummy time is for awake, supervised play.

    When to call the pediatrician

    Call about poor feeding, fewer wet diapers than usual, or a stool that’s bloody, white or clay-colored, or black and tarry after the newborn transition.
